# DWI-053 — product and recent-study coverage: protocol (not executed)

Date: 2026-10-22. State: **planned**. The workpack's coverage register (`workpacks/wes-instrument/data/coverage.json`) stays `scopeComplete: false`; this file does not change that.

## Scope

1. **Product catalogue.** Every Teenage Engineering product page linked from the catalogue (SRC-TE-CATALOG), current and historical, recorded as reviewed, unavailable or out of scope, with the access date. Only functional correspondence is noted (controls, feedback, restraint); no logos, silhouettes, textures or sounds are collected (DWI-002 rights).
2. **Studies.** Primary research on slide design, colour and attention published 2013–2026: assertion–evidence, background colour, signalling and highlighting (SRC-AE2013, SRC-COLOR2024, SRC-SIGNAL2024, SRC-HIGHLIGHT2025 as seeds).

## Search protocol

- Databases: Google Scholar, ERIC, PsycINFO where accessible, publisher pages. Queries recorded verbatim with the date and the number of results screened.
- Inclusion: primary empirical studies with a stated sample and outcome measure. Exclusion: opinion pieces, vendor blogs, secondary summaries.
- For each included study: design, sample size and population, outcome measured, effect as reported (no recomputation), access level (full text, abstract only, paywalled).
- Outcomes are kept apart: **attention** (eye tracking, fixation), **comprehension** (tests), **preference** (ratings) and **persuasion** (attitude change). No outcome is relabelled as another, and no effect size is written that the accessed text does not state.

## What is not claimed

No exhaustive survey, no persuasion score, no universal reading path. SRC-HIGHLIGHT2025 stays abstract-level until its full text is accessed.

## Why not executed in this session

The search is network research on the person's behalf; it was not requested as part of building the application and its results would need a separate review. It is P2 and does not block the local alpha.
