# DWI-060 — formative novice study: protocol (not run)

State: **blocked — needs participants**. No session was held and no result is reported. This protocol fixes the tasks and outcomes before anyone is recruited, so results cannot be chosen afterwards.

## Participants

8–12 people who have made fewer than five presentations in the last year and have not used DeckWeft. Record how they were found, their language (English or Chinese) and whether they use a screen reader or keyboard only. Same material for everyone: two short Markdown notes with one disagreeing number (the DWI-022 fixture).

## Tasks and success criteria (fixed in advance)

| # | Task as told to the participant | Success |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Start a deck from these two notes." | Opens **Review the story** within 3 minutes without help |
| 2 | "Check what the deck will say, then make the slides." | Reads the outline, notices the disagreeing number, makes slides |
| 3 | "Change the headline of page 2." | Correct text on page 2; nothing else changed |
| 4 | "Go to the last page and back to the first." | Uses any route (strip, dial, keys); no page changed by accident |
| 5 | "Take the deck away as a file you can open later." | An HTML or PowerPoint file saved |
| 6 | "Undo your last change; then get back yesterday's version as a copy." | Undo works; restore makes a new deck, the original intact |

## What is recorded, separately

- **Completion and failure causes** per task (where they looked, what they clicked, what they said), not a satisfaction average.
- **Comprehension**: three questions about the deck's content after task 2 (what changed, by how much, which source disagrees).
- **Preference**: one 1–5 rating of the look, kept apart from comprehension. No persuasion or memory claim is made from either.

## Limits to state with any result

Small formative sample; one machine; one material set; English and Chinese reported separately. A larger counterbalanced comparison (dial against plain navigation) is needed before a general claim.
