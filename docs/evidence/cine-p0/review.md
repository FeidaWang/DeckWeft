## Defects found by the evidence and fixed in this batch

- F1 left the first slide of an image-first page without a title (the heading follows the picture in reading order); the pager now reserves the strip on that band and repeats the heading there.
- A repeated heading longer than one line overlapped the band; the continuation title is now sized to one line, or wrapped at the 18 pt floor with the strip reserved for it, in both exporters.
- A PDF band page showed the neighbouring band above its strip; band pages now clip to their band.
- Band cuts used the glyph box of a paragraph while its slide shape takes the line box, so a shape could fall outside its slide; cuts now use the shape box.
- Claim markers at 0.6 em measured as 7 px runs inside body text and failed the typography gate on every compact page; markers are now `max(.7em, 12px)`, written as PowerPoint superscripts and audited at the annotation minimum.
- Two images on an `image-left` page shared one grid cell; the second image now sits in the text column at the foot of the hero.
