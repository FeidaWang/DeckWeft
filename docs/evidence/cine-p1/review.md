## What this batch adds, on the same corpus

The batch 1 corpus (`tests/cine-corpus.mjs`, ten pages in English and Chinese, three synthetic PNGs) gained authored annotations and nothing else: the product hero names its subject (the central 40 % × 40 %), a protected region and a context region and is cropped to the context at aspect 1; the tall detail names the seam as its subject and points at the locator image; the locator shows the whole product; `product-center` is now the evidence screenshot that must not be cropped (`role: product-screenshot`, `factual: true`). 3. **Full sources discoverable, linked, in static outputs.** Unchanged from batch 1: markers, strip (46.2 of 48 px with 12 sources), panel, PowerPoint notes and the PDF appendix; `node --test tests/cine-p0.test.mjs` (Task 6). **Passes.**

5. **References do not divide the canvas into heavy sections.** A run of expanded citations carries one rule (`.regions>.region[citation]+.region[citation]`); compact pages keep the strip. **Passes.**
6. **Reading and presentation targets receive appropriate geometry.** 1440, 1024 and 390 px measured (60 pages, 0 errors); at 390 px every page passes and reads in DOM order; at 1440 the over-budget set is the batch 1 set (product-center, mechanism, overflow, Chinese long-heading); at 1024 also detail (en) and evidence. **Passes with the recorded over-budget pages.**
7. **A sequence retains object identity and finishes in a complete static state.** Not in this batch (CINE-008/009); the product → detail → mechanism pages still share the product asset and the detail now points back at it through the locator. **Not claimed.**
8. **The author can prefer one of the measured alternatives and explain the benefit.** `rankCandidates` states, per loser, every feature that differs from the winner (above); the Studio control for choosing among them is CINE-011. **Passes for the record; no control yet.**
9. **The weakest page has been inspected.** `product-center` viewed at 1440 px and in its two Quick Look bands (`dist/export-targets-cine/decks/cine-en/ql-pptx/slide-03.pptx.png`, `slide-04.pptx.png`); the release gate prints the weakest page per deck (`scripts/release-gate.mjs`, not run in this batch: its decks are the gallery and stories, not the corpus). **Passes.**

## Defects found by the evidence and fixed in this batch

- The 15 px reference role made the 12-source strip 66 px against its 48 px budget and the evidence page 901 px: the strip and its panel keep their 12 px tokens (recorded below the 10 pt target).
- The one-border-per-citation-run rule matched strip items too and padded every strip row by 6 px: scoped to the composition grid.
- After the first stated split the front overflow page, one 80-word paragraph, stopped being dense and rose to 27 px (861 px, past the cut); the dense rule now measures 250 units with CJK glyphs counting two, so a page holding one long paragraph keeps 22 px.
- The runner's "smallest text" of a claim-bearing paragraph was its superscript marker; the evidence reports each region's largest text beside its smallest.
